Plot

In Japan during the era Taisho, Tanjiro Kamado is a boy with a good heart who earns a living selling charcoal
However, his pacific life is destroyed when an Oni kills his whole family.
His younger sister, Nesuko, is the only survivor, but herself is transformed into an Oni!
Tanjiro goes on a dangerous journey to find a way to bring his sister back to normal and destroy the Oni who ruined his life.

About the Hub

Welcome to my website dedicated to the Demon Slayer universe (Kimetsu no Yaiba).
Here you will find a place created for fans that wish to explore the world of the anime in an interactive and creative way. The website brings general information about the series, trivia, and contents specially designed for the community.
The main highlight is the interactive mini-game, where you can discover which Breathing Style suits you best, making the experience unique and fun. In addition, we have a community fan art section, a space dedicated to celebrating fans' creativity, and the fan arts of the month receive special recognition.
